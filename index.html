About us

CK's Collectables LLC is a Kentucky-based business selling Pokémon TCG cards, from raw singles to PSA, CGC and other graded slabs. Every card is checked before it ships. Raw cards go out in a penny sleeve and toploader, and graded slabs are bubble-wrapped in a padded mailer.
